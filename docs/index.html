About the author

Samuel Spera

Samuel Spera is a finance student at Aurora University. He writes about how the bills that come into a home can shape the time a family spends together and the plans people are able to make.

“As someone who is experiencing many of these same pressures, I wanted to write something that connects the numbers we hear about to the lives we live every day.”
